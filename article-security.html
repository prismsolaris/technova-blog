A Simple Checklist for Stronger Online Account Security

A few consistent habits can reduce common risks to your online accounts.

✓

Use unique passwords

A password reused across websites can put several accounts at risk if one service is breached. A reputable password manager can help create and store unique passwords.

Turn on multi-factor authentication

Where available, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA). An authenticator app or a passkey may offer stronger protection than relying on a password alone. Keep recovery methods secure.

Watch for unexpected messages

Be cautious with urgent messages asking you to sign in, pay, or open an attachment. Visit a service by typing its known address or using its official app rather than following a suspicious link.

Keep devices updated

Install operating system, browser, and application updates from official sources. Updates often include security fixes.

Quick review

  • Use unique passwords for important accounts.
  • Enable MFA or passkeys where possible.
  • Review account recovery details.
  • Check active sessions and remove devices you no longer use.
  • Keep software up to date.

Security needs vary by account and situation. For high-risk accounts, follow the service provider's official security guidance.
