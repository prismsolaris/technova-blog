Five Habits for Getting More Useful Results from AI Tools

Better inputs and careful review can make AI tools more helpful.

✳
  1. State the goal. Explain what you want to achieve rather than giving a vague instruction.
  2. Add useful context. Include the audience, format, constraints, and relevant background—without sharing confidential information.
  3. Specify the output. Ask for a checklist, outline, comparison, or concise explanation when that format helps.
  4. Iterate. Review the first response and clarify what should change.
  5. Verify important details. Check facts, sources, calculations, and code before relying on the result.

Keep human judgment involved

AI-generated content can sound confident while being incomplete or incorrect. Treat it as a draft or starting point, especially for technical, financial, legal, medical, and security-related decisions.

AI services have different data policies and capabilities. Review the provider's current terms before using them for sensitive work.
