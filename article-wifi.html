Public Wi-Fi: What to Do Before You Connect

Public networks are convenient. A few checks can help you use them more carefully.

)))

Confirm the network name

Ask staff for the official network name. Avoid joining similarly named networks just because their signal is stronger.

Use secure websites and apps

Look for HTTPS and keep apps updated. A lock icon does not prove a website is trustworthy, so check the domain carefully.

Avoid sensitive activity when unsure

If a network seems suspicious or you cannot verify it, wait until you have a trusted connection before accessing sensitive accounts or making important transactions.

Turn off automatic joining

Disable automatic connection to open networks and remove old public networks from your saved list when you no longer need them.

Use your own connection when appropriate

A mobile hotspot can be a useful alternative. If you use a VPN, choose a reputable provider and understand its privacy practices.

No single step makes an untrusted network completely safe. Follow the security recommendations of the services you use.
